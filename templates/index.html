About Me

Hey everyone! Just a regular Aussie poker player here who got hooked on PPPoker a while back. The games are good, the action is decent, and the community is there. But there's one thing that's always driven me mad: you can't study your hands.

PokerTracker, DriveHUD, GTO Wizard — all the serious training tools need a hand history file. PPPoker doesn't give you one. Their replay system is locked inside the app, and there's no export button in sight. So if you want to actually improve your game, you're stuck replaying hands one by one in a clunky mobile viewer.

I got frustrated enough to do something about it. With the help of AI tools, I built PPPoker Hand Tracker — a web app that pulls your hand history straight from PPPoker's API and converts it into a format that actually works with your training software.

No poker background in software development. No team. Just a player who wanted better tools and figured out how to build them. If it helps your game even a little, it was worth it. ♠

The Tool

PPPoker Hand Tracker bridges the gap between PPPoker and your training software. Here's what it does:

  • Fetches your hands directly from PPPoker's API using your hand review link — no installs, no file uploads, no screen scraping.
  • Converts to PokerStars format — the industry-standard hand history format accepted by PokerTracker 4, DriveHUD, and GTO Wizard.
  • Tracks tournament history — see every tournament you've played, hands logged, session length, and result at a glance.
  • Exports on demand — download all hands, a single hand by ID, or a full tournament in .txt or .json format.

🔒 Your PPPoker account is never touched. This tool works entirely through the public hand review link PPPoker generates for you. It does not ask for, store, or transmit your PPPoker username, password, or any account credentials — ever. The only data processed is the hand history contained in the link you provide.

Conversion accuracy is 95%+ across the hands tested so far. The vast majority of hand types, bet sizes, and board runouts convert cleanly. That said, edge cases exist — unusual all-in situations, incomplete hands, or rare PPPoker-specific action types may not convert perfectly. Use exported files for study and improvement, not as official records.

How to Use

  1. Open PPPoker on your phone or PC Go to your profile → Hand History. You'll see a list of recent hands.
  2. Tap any hand to open the replay Inside the replay screen, look for a Share or Copy Link button. It produces a URL that looks like: http://replay.pppoker.net/...?uid=...&rdkey=...
  3. Paste the link here Drop it into the input box at the top of this page and click Download My Hands. The app fetches up to 200 of your most recent hands — this takes 10–30 seconds.
  4. Review your stats Your session stats, recent hands, and tournament history all load automatically. Check your hole cards, position, and results inline.
  5. Export to your training tool Hit PT4, DriveHUD, or GTO Wizard to download a PokerStars-format .txt file. Import it into your software and start studying.

⚠️ Links expire after a short time. If you get a "link expired" error, go back to PPPoker and copy a fresh link.
